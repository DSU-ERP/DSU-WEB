# Placement Officer Features

This document lists the placement officer features and what each one does.

## 1. Placement Overview

- Select a passing-out batch to view its placement summary.
- View total students, placed students, unplaced students, and placement percentage.
- View average, highest, and median salary packages.
- View total offers, placement drives, and participating companies.
- Compare placement performance across departments.
- View the distribution of Regular, Dream, Super Dream, and Mega placements.
- View leading recruiting companies and recent placements.
- View upcoming and ongoing drives.
- View how many drives are upcoming, ongoing, completed, or cancelled.

## 2. Company Management

- Add recruiting companies.
- Record company name, short name, industry, and website.
- Maintain HR contact name, email, and phone number.
- Search companies and filter by industry.
- Edit company information.
- Mark companies active or inactive.
- View company drive and offer counts.
- Delete companies and their associated placement records after confirmation.

## 3. Placement Drive Management

- Create a drive for a selected company.
- Specify the job role, description, salary package, academic year, and passing-out batch.
- Set minimum CGPA, maximum backlogs, and permitted branches.
- Set the drive date and registration deadline.
- Add, remove, and arrange recruitment rounds.
- Search drives and filter by status or package category.
- Edit drive details and eligibility requirements.
- Change drive status to Upcoming, Ongoing, Completed, or Cancelled.
- Open a drive to view its student pools and recruitment progress.
- Delete drives and their associated records after confirmation.

### Package Categories

| Category | Salary package |
| --- | --- |
| Regular | Up to and including 5 LPA |
| Dream | Above 5 and up to 10 LPA |
| Super Dream | Above 10 and up to 15 LPA |
| Mega | Above 15 LPA |

## 4. Eligibility and Student Registration

- Find students meeting a drive's batch, CGPA, backlog, and branch requirements.
- Identify students already registered for the drive.
- Identify students blocked by their existing placement category.
- Apply the upward-only placement rule: an already placed student can attend a higher package category.
- View Invited, Interested, and Not Interested student pools.
- Register all interested students who have not yet been registered.
- Search and manually select students for registration.
- Use an override to register candidates who have not expressed interest, subject to the selection restrictions shown.
- View invitation and registration stage counts for a drive.

Student interest and drive registration are separate stages; the officer registers candidates before managing their rounds.

## 5. Recruitment Round Tracking

- View the rounds configured for each drive.
- View students awaiting a round and students with recorded results.
- Mark individual round results as Pass or Fail.
- Select multiple students and use Pass All or Fail All.
- View the current round and overall recruitment status of each student.
- Withdraw a student from a drive.
- Select successful candidates and create placement offers.
- Mark a candidate as placed after the final round.

## 6. Offer Management and Placed Students

- View offers with student, department, company, role, package, and category details.
- Filter offers by package category and department.
- View offered, accepted, declined, and revoked offers.
- View active offers and previous offer history.
- Update offer decisions and revoke offers when needed.
- Track a student's current placement category and highest recorded package.
- View placement totals, acceptance counts, department distribution, and leading companies.
- Export the offer list as CSV.

## 7. Company Reports

- View each company's drives for the selected batch.
- View registered and selected candidate totals.
- View active offers, unique hired students, and accepted offers.
- Compare selection and offer acceptance percentages.
- View average and highest packages.
- View the company's package-category distribution.
- Compare recruiting companies by offer count.

## 8. Multiple-Offer Tracking

- Identify students with two or more offers in the selected batch.
- View all their offers, including previous and inactive offers.
- Compare companies, roles, packages, and categories for each student.
- View the student's current category and highest package.
- View total students with multiple offers, maximum offers, and average offers per student.

Multiple-offer history does not necessarily mean that every offer is currently active or accepted.

## 9. Department Placement Intelligence

- Compare departments by placement percentage and package outcomes.
- View total students, students with offers, total offers, and accepted offers.
- View offers per student and offer acceptance percentage.
- Compare minimum, maximum, average, median, 75th-percentile, and 90th-percentile packages.
- View Dream-and-above offers and their share.
- Identify the leading recruiting company for each department.
- Identify departments leading in placement percentage, average package, median package, and highest package.
- Filter by offer status and include or exclude inactive offers.
- Open a department to view its students' individual offer records.

## 10. Individual Student Journey

- Search for a student by name or enrollment number.
- View the student's academic profile and current placement status.
- View every drive they registered for.
- View passed, failed, and absent results already recorded for their rounds.
- View selection and withdrawal history.
- View all company offers and package details.
- Follow a timeline of recruitment activity.
- View drives applied to, rounds cleared, active offers, and highest package.

## 11. Batch Analytics

- Select a passing-out batch and branch.
- View branch-wise student totals and gender distribution.
- View admission-quota distribution, including DSAT, CET, COMEDK, UniGauge, DCET, and Management.
- View CGPA distribution and average CGPA.
- Identify students meeting the baseline eligibility condition: CGPA at least 6 with no current backlogs.
- Open a branch's student list.
- Search students and filter by quota or CGPA.
- Expand a student to view academic, admission, contact, and family details.
- Export batch summaries and branch student lists as CSV.

Baseline eligibility is a general academic measure; individual drives can have different requirements.

## 12. Academic Student Categories

| Category | CGPA |
| --- | --- |
| Advanced | 8.5 and above |
| Medium | 6.75 to below 8.5 |
| Needs Improvement | Below 6.75 |

- View student counts in each category.
- Open a category to inspect its students and academic details.
- Compare student ranks and baseline eligibility.
- Export category student lists as CSV.

These categories describe academic performance and are separate from placement-test classifications.

## 13. CGPA Ranking

- Rank students by CGPA.
- View college rank and department rank.
- Select batch and department.
- Filter eligible or ineligible students.
- View CGPA, SGPA, backlogs, attendance, and eligibility.
- Export the ranking as CSV.

## 14. Master Placement Test Results

- View completed Tier 1 and Tier 2 placement-test results.
- View scores, percentages, time taken, and Tier 2 qualification.
- View Basic, Medium, and Advanced assessment classifications.
- Compare students by combined score and rank.
- Search by student name, enrollment number, or email.
- Filter by department, classification, and violation group.
- Expand students to review reported exam security indicators.
- View tab switches, fullscreen exits, focus loss, copy/paste attempts, and other reported indicators.
- Identify records flagged for review and auto-submitted attempts.
- View participation, average scores, and violation summaries.
- Refresh results and browse paginated student lists.

This screen displays results and review indicators; it does not provide a completed test-creation or review-approval workflow.

## 15. Points, GitHub, and Domain Rankings

### Points Ranking

- Rank students by total points.
- View academic, GitHub, and domain-test contributions.
- Filter by department.
- Expand a student to inspect the points breakdown.
- Refresh ranking information.

### GitHub Ranking

- Rank students by GitHub points.
- View identity, department, GitHub score, and total points.
- Refresh the list.

### Domain Ranking

- Rank students by domain-test points.
- View identity, department, domain score, and total points.
- Refresh the list.

## 16. Talent Intelligence

- View students' existing career predictions alongside academics and points.
- View recommended career paths, domains, strengths, risks, and recommendations.
- View alignment, practical, theoretical, and course-fit scores where available.
- Group students into Job-Ready, Mid-Level, and Entry-Level readiness levels.
- View the distribution of suggested career roles.
- Find potential candidates using role, domain, or skill keywords.
- Search the student matrix and filter by department, readiness, risk, or programming language.
- Sort by points, CGPA, or alignment score.
- Expand individual student profiles for detailed insights.
- View students identified with higher career risk.
- View programming-language distribution.
- Identify students who have not generated career predictions.
- Export the filtered talent matrix as CSV.
- Refresh insights and browse paginated records.

The prediction-coverage window identifies missing predictions; it does not generate predictions for students in bulk.

## 17. Observation Haki Assistant

- Ask questions through text chat.
- Look up a student's academic and available profile information.
- Search registered students by name.
- Search the web for industry information and job opportunities.
- Draft student emails and confirm sending through chat.
- Start a new chat.
- View, open, and delete available conversation history.
- Use suggested prompt cards to begin a question.

Current limits: new officer chat messages are not saved into the available history, and several placement-themed suggestions do not have a corresponding working placement-report feature.

## 18. F.R.I.D.A.Y. AI Assistant

- Submit instructions through text or voice.
- Receive spoken responses and replay them.
- Look up academic student profiles.
- Request academic batch summaries and CGPA-based candidate lists.
- Request recent drive information.
- Search the web for hiring and industry information when search is available.
- Use quick prompts for common requests.
- View and clear the current session's conversation log.
- Enable or disable the microphone and spoken responses.
- View proposed actions with confirm or cancel controls.
- Refresh the assistant's diagnostics display.

Current limits: several skills, placement-statistics, and test-ranking requests currently return academic information instead of the advertised analysis. Proposed drive creation, exam scheduling, announcement emails, and placement-status updates are incomplete in the current assistant flow. Session history resets when the page reloads.

## 19. Reports and Downloads

Available CSV downloads include:

- Batch analytics summaries.
- Branch student lists.
- Academic category student lists.
- CGPA rankings.
- Placement offer lists.
- Filtered talent intelligence lists.

## 20. Navigation and General Controls

- Expand or collapse the sidebar.
- Switch between placement dashboard sections.
- Open either AI assistant and return to the dashboard.
- Select batches within the relevant reports.
- Switch the selected theme.
- Sign out.

## 21. Features Shown but Not Complete

- **Student Progress:** currently has no populated student list; displayed attempt and offer counts in its unfinished view are sample values.
- **General Export Data:** currently uses the unfinished student list and can produce an empty report.
- **Notification bell:** displayed without a working officer notification feed.
- **Settings:** the menu entry has no working officer settings screen.
- **Bulk career prediction:** displays students missing predictions but does not run predictions.
- **Master Test review actions:** indicators are visible, but approving reviews or changing classifications is not available here.
- **F.R.I.D.A.Y. action execution:** the proposed-action controls do not yet form a reliable completed workflow.
