## Scope

Landing page `/` (`src/app/page.tsx`, `CampusPortal`, `Navbar`, `src/app/landing.css`). Mode: Persuade, as a pure sign-in gateway. Sign-in, sign-up and the portals are out of scope.

## Audience and job

DSU students, placement officers and invited recruiters arriving from the university, the placement office or an invitation email. Job: recognise DSU's own system and get into sign-in in one step; new students find account creation; recruiters learn they join by invitation.

## Constraints

Owner-pinned palette: charcoal and sand. Must keep: the campus illustration, "ONE CAMPUS. / INFINITE POSSIBILITIES.", the pinned scroll morph into the access portal, and the official DSU seal. No invented claims, dates or dignitaries on the stone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The gateway is the campus's own dedication stone: polished charcoal granite with sand-gilt lettering cut into it. It refuses the category's split hero of a headline, a login card and a stock building photo.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal granite ground with a faint fleck and a specular sheen; sand gilt (#D4BF96) is the only ink, with darker sand for secondary lines. Wide Archivo capitals, tracked like plaque lettering, with an incised edge. The official seal is carved as a sand roundel, and the campus drawing is etched into the stone. Buttons are square gilt plates; rules are hairline gilt. No other colour except the official logo lockup in the header.

STORY: The visitor recognises DSU (seal, building, name), reads one line, and signs in. New students see "Create account"; recruiters see that access comes by invitation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The header carries the official light logo on the left and a Sign in link on the right. Down a centred axis: the carved seal roundel (~112px), "ONE CAMPUS." at full plaque scale, "INFINITE POSSIBILITIES." widely tracked, then the Sign in gilt plate beside an outlined Create account plate. The etched building rises from the bottom edge across ~70% of the width.

FORM: Foundation stone, IMPECCABLE'S PICK, position 1 on the ordered list. seed:aecd11e61b5f36de. Signature interaction: the scroll morph is one linked pull. The inscription lifts away, the etched building slides right into a giant carved seal ring, and the ACCESS PORTAL inscription is revealed on the left, while the light on the polished stone moves with the same progress.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
